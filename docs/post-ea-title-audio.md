# Post-EA title: Ghidra/audio notes

## What the original ROM does

The SNES version does not stream a finished song. `$82:AC0E` selects the post-EA
resource list at `$82:A9D1/$82:AB69`. `$82:ABE0` walks that list and
`$82:ABCE -> $80:9829` transfers the selected sequence, instrument, and BRR
sample resources through APU ports `$2140-$2143` into SPC RAM. The resident
SPC700 sound driver sequences those short samples on the SNES audio voices.

The title visuals are synchronized by cue values delivered to CPU RAM `$064A`.
`$80:E381` consumes and clears each cue. The following blocks perform the
observed build:

- `$80:E391`: cue 1/7, N stage
- `$80:E3DB`: cue 2/8, B stage
- `$80:E432`: cue 3/9, A stage
- `$80:E489`: cue 4/10/11, LIVE stage
- `$80:E4E0`: cue 5, 95 stage
- `$80:E548`: cue 6, enable the lights around 95
- `$80:E8D9`: every eight frames, rotate three CGRAM colors used by the lights

`$87:8000/$87:80CB` belong to the timeout-to-attract transition. They do not
construct the initial title letters. `$87:80CB` moves the credit layer through
the shadow registers at `$0615/$1872`, uses `$186C` as the credit index, and
`$186E` as its delay/hold counter.

## What the current C port does

The recorded title WAV and rendered-video stream were removed. The versioned
asset pack now contains typed hardware resources:

- 80/81: the title's 64 KiB VRAM and 512-byte CGRAM origin
- 82: frame-stamped PPU registers plus raw VRAM/CGRAM writes (`NBTPPU1`)
- 83/84/85: SPC RAM, DSP registers, and SPC700 register file (`NBTSPC1`)
- 86: the 65816-to-APU `$2140-$2143` command stream (`NBTAPU1`)
- 87: the `$064A` music/visual cue stream (`NBTCUE1`)

`src/nba_spc.c` executes the ROM's SPC700 driver and synthesizes its BRR sample
bank through the port's S-DSP implementation at 32 kHz. `src/nba_audio.c`
delivers the captured CPU command writes at their original frame boundaries and
builds the host playback buffer at runtime. The asset pack therefore contains
no mixed title song.

`src/nba_title_sequence.c` is a Mode-1 software PPU. It decodes the original
planar tiles and tilemaps, applies per-tile priority, CGRAM, scroll registers,
INIDISP brightness, palette animation, and the later credit movement. The pack
contains no screenshots or rendered pixels.

The credit renderer also reproduces the raster split seen in
`tools/mesen_title_raster_trace.lua`: BG3 is enabled at scanline `$90`, the
role line uses the fixed `$FFAD` vertical scroll, and the lower name band uses
the `$1872` scroll written later in the frame. This is why both credit lines
remain above the gym floor exactly as in the ROM.

The authoritative state is generated by `tools/mesen_title_capture.lua` from
the user's ROM. Mesen is a reverse-engineering/capture tool only and is not a
runtime dependency. `tools/test_title_pipeline.py` rejects the retired asset
formats, validates the six primary cue frames (235, 469, 586, 703, 820, 938),
checks the later 7/8/9/10 credit cues, verifies all 43,777 APU writes, and locks
Mesen-matched renderer frames with RGB hashes.

The 52 standalone WAVs in F11 remain BRR previews for debugging. They are not
the title playlist; title playback uses the SPC hardware assets above.
